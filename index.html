PROFILE

About Me

I am Dr. Majid N. V., Assistant Professor of Mathematics at Mar Athanasius College (Autonomous), Kothamangalam, Kerala. I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics at Pondicherry University. My doctoral research focused on the theory of partitions and mock theta functions.

My academic interests span analytic number theory, integer partitions, q-series, mock theta functions, modular forms, algebraic combinatorics and graph theory. Alongside research, I contribute to undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, student mentoring and outreach activities through the National Service Scheme.
